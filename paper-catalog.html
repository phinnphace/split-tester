03 / Reading the catalog

Structure before interpretation

What was recorded

Each registered citation receives the same 21-field protocol: source availability, datasets named and used, split and independence, preprocessing, architecture, optimizer or novelty, hardware and software, outputs, consistency, tested-versus-reported construct, baselines, ablation, code, data, ethics, claim support, and source-bound notes.

What the map does not do

  • It does not infer misconduct from cluster membership.
  • It does not convert missing reporting into evidence of absence.
  • It does not treat an automated extraction as final adjudication.
  • Papers may belong to multiple clusters because the grouping is compositional.

Snapshot: 12 August 2026. Forty-four full texts and one citation-only dataset record. The catalog will evolve as reproducibility checks and independent review are added.
